## Part 2 — Where the Money Is

#### Tier A — Highest compensation, hardest entry
**Enterprise automation consulting** — replacing manual Excel/email processes with Python, scheduled tasks, and internal tools. This is exactly what you built at D&H. Companies pay ₹8,000–₹25,000/day for this work. Entry requires either referrals or a proven track record on platforms.

**AI/LLM tooling** — building MCP servers, RAG pipelines, LLM-integrated workflows for companies trying to adopt Claude or GPT internally. The Balance Sheet MCP server is directly relevant here. Market is young, undercrowded, and pricing hasn't compressed yet. Rates: $80–$150/hr on Western platforms.

#### Tier B — Reliable, good margin
**Data pipeline and reporting automation** — Python + pandas + Excel/Power BI output for SMBs that don't have a data team. Your openpyxl, SSRS, and BigMint tracker experience maps here exactly. Rates: $40–$80/hr.

**Scraping and data acquisition** — commercial scrapers, price trackers, competitive intelligence pipelines. Your pywebview + requests + BeautifulSoup work is the portfolio. Rates: $35–$70/hr, or ₹2,000–₹6,000/project on Indian platforms.

#### Tier C — Entry volume, lower margin
**Python scripting and automation** — Upwork bottom-of-funnel work. Useful for reviews and initial credibility. Not where you want to stay. Rates: $15–$35/hr. Do 3–5 jobs here, then move up.

## Part 3 — Service Positioning

Don't position as "Python developer." That describes ten million people.

**Primary positioning:** *Enterprise workflow automation — turning manual processes into scheduled, auditable Python systems.*

This framing:
- Speaks to the buyer's pain (manual work, errors, time wasted)
- Is specific enough to filter out tire-kickers
- Matches your real experience precisely
- Commands higher rates than "I write Python scripts"

**Secondary positioning (for AI-era clients):** *Internal AI tooling — MCP servers, LLM-integrated pipelines, and Claude/GPT integrations for business workflows.*

This is your forward edge. Almost nobody has shipped a real MCP server. You have one in production.

**What NOT to say:** "I do web development," "I know machine learning," "I can build anything." Generalist positioning competes on price. Specialist positioning competes on fit.

## Part 4 — Platform Strategy

#### Upwork
Start here. Most accessible. Indian developers compete heavily on price — your goal is to escape that bucket by positioning on outcomes, not hours.

**Profile setup:**
- Title: *Enterprise Automation Engineer — Python, Playwright, FastAPI*
- Overview: Open with a specific result. "I built a scheduled automation platform managing 21 scripts for a manufacturing company — live in production, 300+ daily automated actions." Then explain what you do and who you do it for.
- Hourly rate: Start at $25/hr. Raise $5 every 3 successful jobs until market resistance. Target: $50–$75/hr within 12 months.
- Portfolio: Link rasmnama.com. Screenshot the Orchestration Portal dashboard. Write short case studies for BigMint tracker and Rate Approval bot.
- Availability: Set to "More than 30 hours/week" initially — algorithm favors available profiles.

**First 3 jobs strategy:** Bid below your target rate. Win jobs with detailed, specific proposals. Deliver exceptional. Collect 5-star reviews. Speed matters here — respond to job postings within 2 hours of posting.

#### Toptal
Higher bar. They interview and test. Apply after 6 months of Upwork history and 3+ strong reviews. If you pass, rates start at $60/hr and average $90–$120/hr for automation work. Worth the effort.

**Prep for Toptal screen:** Practice LeetCode medium problems (algorithmic screen), prepare to walk through a real system you built (technical interview), review system design basics (scalability, database design). The Balance Sheet + MCP work is strong material here.

#### LinkedIn
Not a job board — a credibility surface. Post 2x/week. Content that works:
- "Here's how I automated X at a manufacturing company — before/after"
- Technical posts explaining something specific (pywebview cookie capture, FastMCP dual deployment)
- Behind-the-scenes on rasmnama.com development

Connect with CTOs and VPs of Operations at mid-market manufacturing, pharma, and trading companies. That's your buyer. Don't pitch immediately — post, comment, be visible.

#### Indian-specific platforms
Workhall, Flexiple, Pepper Content (for tech writing). Lower rates but lower competition and faster first jobs. Use for cash flow while building Upwork profile. Target ₹1,500–₹3,000/hr on Flexiple for automation work.

#### Direct outreach
After 6 months: email 5 companies/week. Target: small-to-mid manufacturing, pharma distribution, trading firms in India with no data team. Subject line: "Reducing manual Excel work at [Company Name]." Reference a specific problem you know they likely have. This is your highest-margin path — no platform fees.

## Part 5 — Portfolio Assets to Build Now

Your portfolio.datawithaditya.github.io is good. You need 3 more things:

#### 1. Case study documents
For each major project, write a 1-page PDF: Problem → What I built → Result (specific numbers). These go to direct outreach clients. They don't read GitHub.

Priority order:

- Rate Approval bot (directly relatable for anyone with approval workflows)

#### 2. A demo video
3–5 minute Loom recording of the Orchestration Portal or RasmNama. Show it working. Narrate what it's doing. Post it on LinkedIn and embed in your Upwork profile. Video converts significantly better than text.

#### 3. One public OSS contribution
Find a popular Python library (FastMCP, APScheduler, Playwright) and fix a bug or add a small feature. One merged PR to a respected repo signals that you write production-quality code. Takes 1–3 days.

## Part 6 — Pricing and Rates

**Never quote hourly if you can avoid it.** Fixed-price projects protect your income when you're efficient. An automation that takes you 3 days to build is worth ₹30,000–₹50,000 to the client — not 3 days × your hourly rate.

**Rate card (internal reference — do not publish):**

| Service | Rate |
|---|---|
| Python automation script (simple) | ₹8,000–₹15,000 flat |
| Desktop tool with Tkinter + PyInstaller | ₹20,000–₹40,000 flat |
| Web scraper + data pipeline | ₹15,000–₹35,000 flat |
| Flask/FastAPI backend (small) | ₹25,000–₹60,000 flat |
| MCP server integration | $500–$2,000 flat |
| Hourly (Upwork) | $25/hr → $75/hr (12-month target) |
| Monthly retainer (maintenance/support) | ₹15,000–₹40,000/month |

**Retainers are your goal.** One client paying ₹25,000/month for ongoing support is better than four one-time projects at ₹25,000 each — same revenue, 75% less sales effort.

- Apply to Toptal if you have 5+ reviews.

#### Months 7–9 — Direct client push
- Reduce Upwork hours. Start converting direct outreach into primary pipeline.

#### Months 10–12 — Review and scale decision
Evaluate three options:

**Option A — Solo freelancer at scale.** 2–3 retainer clients + selective project work. Revenue: ₹1,20,000–₹2,00,000/month. Freedom maximised. Growth limited.

**Option B — Productise.** Build a vertical SaaS (manufacturing operations tool, procurement automation platform). RasmNama is proof you can do this. Revenue ceiling: much higher. Risk: higher.

**Option C — Agency.** Subcontract to 1–2 junior developers. Take larger enterprise contracts. Revenue: ₹3,00,000–₹6,00,000/month at scale. Overhead: significant.

The answer depends on what you want. Make this decision at month 10, not now.

## Part 8 — Financial Planning

**Before going full-time freelance:**
- 6 months of expenses saved. Non-negotiable.
- At least 1 paying client confirmed (not "interested" — confirmed, contracted, first payment received).
- Know your minimum viable monthly revenue: rent + food + EMIs + 30% tax buffer.

**Tax:**
- Register under GST once revenue exceeds ₹20 lakh/year (threshold may change — verify).
- File ITR as professional income (44ADA presumptive taxation scheme — 50% of gross receipts taxed if below ₹75 lakh, no books required). Consult a CA before filing year 1.
- Invoice international clients in USD/EUR. Receive via Wise or direct SWIFT. Declare as export of services — 0% GST on foreign clients.

**Tools:**
- Wise account for USD receipts (better rates than Indian banks)
- Razorpay for Indian client invoices
- Wave (free) or Zoho Books for invoicing and expense tracking
- Separate current account for freelance income — never mix with salary

**Pricing floor:** Know the minimum hourly rate below which it's not worth taking a project. Factor in Upwork's 20% fee on first $500 per client, 10% thereafter. At $25/hr gross, you net $20/hr after Upwork cut.

## Part 9 — Parallel Track — While Still at D&H

You don't need to quit to start. The correct sequence:

1. **Now:** Build Upwork profile. Start applying. Complete first 3 jobs on evenings/weekends.
2. **Month 3–4:** First paid client. Confirm you can deliver while employed.
3. **Month 6:** Assess revenue trajectory. Is it on track for ₹80,000+/month within 6 more months?
4. **Month 9–12:** If yes, give notice. Leave on good terms — D&H could become a client.

The riskiest move is quitting before you have proof of demand. The second riskiest is waiting too long and never starting.

**On moonlighting:** Check your employment contract. Most Indian employment contracts prohibit "competitive employment" not "additional professional activity." Automation work for non-manufacturing-sector clients is unlikely to conflict. When in doubt, avoid taking D&H's direct competitors as clients while employed.

## Part 10 — Failure Modes to Avoid

**Undercharging and staying there.** If you never raise rates, clients will never expect you to. Build in a rate review every 3 months.

**Taking every project.** A $15/hr project from a difficult client costs more than it earns. Qualify clients. If they haggle before the project starts, they'll dispute invoices after.

**Building without selling.** More portfolio projects do not replace proposals. The market doesn't automatically find you. Sales activity (proposals, posts, outreach) must happen every week, not just when you need work.

**Scope creep without contracts.** Every project needs a written scope: what's included, what's not, how changes are priced. A WhatsApp message confirming scope counts as a contract in Indian law but a signed PDF is better.

**Burning out in month 2.** Freelancing while employed is genuinely hard. Limit yourself to 10–15 hours/week maximum. One solid project per month is enough to build a track record. You don't need to sprint the whole year.

**Waiting for perfect.** The portfolio is good enough. The skills are real. The only thing missing is starting. Send the first proposal today.
